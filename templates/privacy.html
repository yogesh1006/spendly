Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking service (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register for an account. We also collect the expense data you choose to log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. Basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, may be collected automatically to help us operate the Service.

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide and improve the Service, including displaying your expense history, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account. We may also use your email address to send important account or service-related notices. We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting, which may process data on our behalf solely to help us operate the Service, and only to the extent necessary to do so.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help.
